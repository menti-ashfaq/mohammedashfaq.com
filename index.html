How the pick works

  • Staleness, squared. Every dish gets a weight of (days since cooked + 1)², so a meal you last made in spring easily outbids last Tuesday's pasta.
  • Never-cooked counts as 90 days. New dishes compete hard without completely taking over the rotation.
  • Shuffles cheat, on purpose. Four times in ten a reroll ignores the weighting entirely, so it never feels like the app is on rails.
  • Nothing leaves the device. No accounts, no backend, no analytics — in the app, and on this page too. Your taps here stay in your own browser.
